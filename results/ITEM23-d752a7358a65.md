## Verdict

- **Speed vs balanced: faster, but not shippable as is - correctness failure on S3 small queries at 32 clients**
  (ADBC 702-753 and JDBC 66-91 of 1280 queries fail or return different answers, every round; balanced 0). Also
  slower tails on S3 at 8 clients (p95/p99 1.38-1.64 in every round) and small local single-client p50 (ADBC 1.06,
  local ADBC c8 p95 1.15). Gains where correct: concurrent small-query p50 at 8/32 clients 37-50 % lower, scan x8
  +36-43 %, closed-loop load +11-22 % at 8-128 clients, TPC-H 2-7 % faster locally and ~5 % on S3 SF1.
- **Where speed reaches or passes upstream native:** local scans x1/x4 (1.13 / 1.06) and export (1.04) at 24 % of
  upstream's RSS (883 vs 3677 MB); slow link with ZSTD level (1.01); closed-loop load at 1 client (1.18) and within
  4-10 % at 8-128 clients (JDBC 0.96, ADBC 0.90 at 128) at 84 % of upstream's RSS. Not reached: S3 parallel scans
  (x4 0.70) and S3 export (0.48). TPC-H and small queries: upstream fails the correctness gate, so no ranking.
